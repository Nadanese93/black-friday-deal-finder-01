How these prices were checked

Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026 from the store's own product page or from a deals source that names the store — the source is printed on each deal. The discount shown is always computed from the two prices on the card, so it adds up. A NEW LOW means the lowest price tracked for that product this season; STRONG means a deep cut near its best price; ROUTINE means a modest cut, or a saving measured against a list price the product rarely sold at. Prices move fast on Black Friday — always confirm the price on the store page before you buy. Links go straight to the product at the store named.
